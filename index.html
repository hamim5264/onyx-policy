Onyx Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 17, 2026

Welcome to Onyx ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Onyx (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with our expense tracking features, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign up via email/password or use Google/Facebook Social Login, we collect your name, email address, profile picture (if applicable), and user ID.
  • Financial Data: This includes expenses, transaction details, and wallets you manually log or sync inside the App.

SMS Permission & Data Usage: Onyx includes an optional feature to automatically sync your financial transactions by reading incoming SMS messages. We ONLY scan transactional SMS messages (such as debit/credit alerts from your bank) locally on your device to extract transaction details (amount, vendor, timestamp). We do NOT read your personal conversations, and we do NOT upload your raw SMS text to any third-party servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To set up and manage your secure user account.
  • To track, categorize, and present your expenses and income data.
  • To automatically extract transaction information from bank SMS alerts (if SMS Sync is enabled by you).
  • To secure your data using our Supabase backend infrastructure.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data processed by Onyx is secured and stored using Supabase, our cloud database provider. We utilize industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your personal and financial information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Integrations

Our App integrates with the following third-party services for authentication:

  • Google Sign-In: Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook Login (Meta): Subject to Meta's Data Policy.

5. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. If you wish to delete your Onyx account and permanently remove all your data from our database, you can do so directly in the App settings, or by visiting our User Data Deletion Instructions page.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date.
