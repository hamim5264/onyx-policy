Onyx - Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

At Onyx, we value your privacy and control over your personal data. According to Meta (Facebook) and Google Play Platform rules, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and any associated data synced from third-party social integrations (like Facebook Login).

Here are the step-by-step instructions on how to request the deletion of your user data:

Method 1: In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

You can instantly delete all your data directly inside the App:

  1. Open the Onyx app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (person icon in the bottom menu).
  3. Select Login and security (or Data and privacy).
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

*Note: This will instantly and permanently delete your profile, transaction logs, and wallet configurations from our database.

Method 2: Email Data Deletion Request

If you cannot access the App or wish to request deletion manually, you can email us:

  1. Send an email to: hamim.leon@gmail.com
  2. Subject line: Onyx Data Deletion Request
  3. In the email body, provide your registered email address or your Facebook User ID associated with the App.

Upon receiving your email, we will permanently purge all your profile information, social tokens, transactional logs, and sync configurations from our servers within 48 hours. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

What Data is Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following information is permanently destroyed from our Supabase database:

  • Your user account profile (Name, email address, avatar photo).
  • All logged income, expenses, and budget plans.
  • All connected wallet information.
  • Saved security questions and answer hashes.
